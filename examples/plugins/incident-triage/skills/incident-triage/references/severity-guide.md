# Sample severity guide

Use the organization's own incident policy when one is available. This sample is only a discussion aid.

| Level | Example impact |
| --- | --- |
| SEV-1 | A critical customer-facing service is broadly unavailable, or safety, security, or data integrity may be at risk. |
| SEV-2 | A major feature or region is significantly degraded, with no practical workaround for many users. |
| SEV-3 | A limited feature or user group is affected, or a workaround is available. |
| SEV-4 | Minor impact, an isolated defect, or a question that does not currently disrupt service. |

Do not infer severity from the label alone. Record the observed scope, duration, and workaround status, then let the incident owner apply the relevant policy.
